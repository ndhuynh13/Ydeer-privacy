Privacy Policy

Sketch Music (com.ydeer.sketchmusic) · Developer: Ydeer · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sketch Music app ("the App") handles your information. The App is developed by Ydeer ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

The App works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not send your data to any server.

Information the App accesses

  • Camera – only when you choose to take a photo to use as a drawing background. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photos you pick – when you choose an image from your gallery as a background, the App reads only the image you select, using the Android photo picker.
  • Files you save – drawings and music you export are saved to your device storage (for example Pictures or Downloads) only when you ask to save them.
  • App settings – preferences such as the light/dark theme and your projects are stored locally on your device.

None of this information leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself, for example with the Share button. Shared content is then handled by the app you share it to, under that app's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

All data is kept only on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting projects in the App, clearing the App's data in Android Settings, or uninstalling the App.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We do not transmit or store your data elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
