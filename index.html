Privacy Policy

Sketch Music (com.ydeer.sketchmusic) · Developer: Ydeer · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sketch Music app ("the App") handles your information. The App is developed by Ydeer ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • Your drawings, music and projects stay on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect them.
  • The App has no user accounts.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Premium purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information the App accesses on your device

  • Camera – only when you choose to take a photo to use as a drawing background. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photos you pick – when you choose an image from your gallery as a background, the App reads only the image you select, using the Android photo picker.
  • Files you save – drawings and music you export are saved to your device storage (for example Pictures or Downloads) only when you ask to save them.
  • App settings – preferences such as the light/dark theme and your projects are stored locally on your device.

None of this content leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself, for example with the Share button. Shared content is then handled by the app you share it to, under that app's own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalize (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and send to Google:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data, such as ad views and taps, and app performance and crash data related to ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the App's settings ("Privacy choices").
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Buying Premium removes ads from the App.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and is never shared with us. The App only receives confirmation of which products you own, so it can unlock Premium features. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Apart from the data collected by Google for ads and purchases described above, the App does not share any information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your projects and settings are kept only on your device. You can delete them at any time by deleting projects in the App, clearing the App's data in Android Settings, or uninstalling the App. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's own policies; you can reset your advertising ID as described above.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security

Your projects stay on your device and are protected by your device's own security. Data sent to Google for ads and purchases is transmitted using encryption in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
